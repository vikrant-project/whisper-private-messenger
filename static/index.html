Security Model & Realistic Limits

Cryptographic Assurances:
  • End-to-End Encryption: Only you and your recipient possess the keys to decrypt message contents.
  • Forward Secrecy: Ephemeral Diffie-Hellman ratcheting ensures past messages cannot be decrypted even if keys are compromised later.
  • Device Ownership Binding: Usernames cannot be spoofed or claimed without the authentic Ed25519 identity key.
Honest Limitations & What Cannot Be Protected:
  • Compromised Endpoint: If your computer, phone, or browser has malware, screen recorders, or physical intrusion, encryption in memory cannot defend against it.
  • Connection Metadata: The server routes packets and therefore observes IP addresses, timestamps, and message frequencies. We do not claim "100% anonymity." For network traffic masking, combine with Tor or a trusted VPN.
  • Device Storage Loss: If you clear browser cookies or discard your device without an encrypted backup file, your identity is unrecoverable.
